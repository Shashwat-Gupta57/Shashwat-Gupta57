The whole policy

  • I don’t know who you are. And I’m keeping it that way.
  • No cookies. Not even the chocolate kind.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no ads. I genuinely can’t tell how many people saw this. Ego says a lot.
  • No third parties. Even the fonts live on this site, so no big company finds out you dropped by.
  • No forms, no sign-ups. The only thing you “signed” was a joke.

That’s it. That’s the policy.

Why Talken is different

Every other messenger asks you to trust them. Talken just doesn’t give anyone anything to hold.

  • Triple-wrapped. A message gets locked three times before it leaves your device. Peel one layer, there’s another. Peel that, there’s another.
  • More private than Tor. Tor bounces you through three strangers’ computers and hopes none of them are watching. Talken skips the strangers: it goes straight from you to your friend, peer to peer.
  • No server in the middle. There’s no message server to hack, leak, subpoena or “ask nicely”. You can’t hand over what you never had.
  • No phone number. Your key is your account. Good luck tracing that to a SIM card.
  • Your files, your Drive. Media goes to your own storage, not mine.

Dear governments: you can knock, but there’s no door. 👋

The boring bit

Okay, to be fully honest: this site runs on GitHub Pages, and GitHub keeps normal server logs like every web host does. I can’t see them. Their privacy statement covers that.

Your browser remembers two things, on your device only: sg-theme (light or dark) and sg-privacy (so the “sign here” thing doesn’t bug you twice). Clear your site data and they’re gone.

Downloads come from GitHub Releases. Talken has its own proper policy. The weather widgets talk to WeatherAPI for the forecast. Nothing else here phones home.
